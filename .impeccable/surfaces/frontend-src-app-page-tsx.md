# Surface brief: FSB Nexus web app (landing + app shell)

Scope: landing (Persuade) and the signed-in app (Operate: chat, courses, dashboard, onboarding, profile, admin). Audience: FSB students, mostly on phones; staff for admin. Action: ask a question and get a sourced answer.

## Direction contract
Seed key: da94eaaa (assigned)

THESIS: The faculty as a louage station: every question boards a line and arrives with its ticket (the source). Refuses the blue SaaS split hero and the neon AI terminal.

OWN-WORLD: Van-white ground, near-black ink, one louage-red band as structure; hand-painted bilingual placards (Lalezar display, Arabic and French side by side); Rubik UI; perforated ticket-stub chips for sources. Night station dark mode: asphalt ground, sodium-amber secondary.

STORY: Students recognise the station; they understand "pick your line, ask, keep your ticket" without explanation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Station board of four line placards above a working question field ("Où vas-tu ?"); one real example answer with ticket stubs, labelled as an example.

SIGNATURE: Citations print as ticket stubs that open the source passage.

RISK: Theme-park overreach. Metaphor lives only in placards, stubs and the red band; the chat stays plain and fast.
